Where the area goes
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  vte_axil_regs     storage dominates: 16 palette entries of 12 bits plus
                    about 100 bits of configuration, the 32 bit read mux and
                    the read-modify-write path for byte strobes.
  vte_glyph_rom     3072 bytes of constant data mapped to gates because the
                    generic library has no memory primitive. On an FPGA or
                    with a technology library this is one block RAM.
  vte_cdc_fifo      32 entries of 19 bits as distributed registers, plus the
                    gray coded pointers and both synchronisers.
  vte_timing_gen    two 12 bit position counters, the region comparators and
                    the grid clamping arithmetic.
  vte_frame_sync    two more copies of the configuration bundle, one frozen
                    shadow in the register domain and one live in the pixel
                    domain. That is the price of atomic reconfiguration.

No inferred latches and no black boxes.
