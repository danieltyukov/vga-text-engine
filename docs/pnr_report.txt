  integrates the block, not a functional defect.

  The cells grew by 94650 um2 between synthesis and routing, and
  73282 um2 of that is timing repair buffers with another 13929 um2
  of clock tree: 92 percent of the growth.

  That is the same story the synthesis timing report tells from the other
  end. Synthesis left the enables that gate the configuration snapshot
  driven by minimum size gates into several hundred loads, and place and
  route spent a quarter of the cell area buffering them. The setup slack
  improved from +4.965 ns at synthesis level to
  +7.585 ns after routing, at the same 15.3846 ns pixel clock.

  Quote the die area if only one number is quoted. Fill cells are not
  logic, so the honest comparison against a synthesis estimate is the
  standard cell figure.
